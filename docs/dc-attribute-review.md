# DC attribute review (Version 2, 2026-11-27)

The DC pool was seeded from archetype templates (`data/new-pool-core.js`): every `tactician` started as the same planner with an ordinary body, every `metahuman` with Strength 93, every `deity` with Size 97. Batman was stored at Speed 67, Agility 72 and Strength 64 and lost to John Wick 96% of the time; Poison Ivy and Black Canary were as strong as Aquaman; Wonder Woman was larger than the Hulk.

This review corrects the entries where the character clearly contradicts the template, on the same 1–99 scale as the hand-authored Marvel pool (Captain America 84/93/88 with Size 70, Black Widow Size 50, Thor Size 85). Each row lists only the attributes changed. They are editorial ratings of the classic comics identity, not canonical measurements. The source is the character's entry in the Version 2 research record.

Version 1 is not changed. The nine cosmic entities (Trigon, Anti-Monitor, the Spectre, Mister Mxyzptlk, Doctor Manhattan, Lucifer, Dream, Death, Perpetua) and Darkseid, Doomsday, Ares and Brainiac are left as they are: their balance rests on the owner's cosmic rulings of 2026-11-27.
